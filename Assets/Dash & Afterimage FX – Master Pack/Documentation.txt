Setup:

1. Drag one of the AfterimageController scripts from the Scripts/ folder onto your character.
2. Create a GameObject and attach one of the effects from the Scripts/Effects/ folder to it.
3. Assign your GameObject in the AfterimageController component.
4. Customize the material and effect settings as you like.

VFX:
1. Drag one of the AfterimageController scripts from the Scripts/ folder onto your character.
2. Create a GameObject and attach the VFXEffect script from the Scripts/Effects/ folder to it.
3. Add a VFX Graph to the GameObject (demo effects are in the Effects/Visual Effects/ folder).
4. Create a Property Binder and assign your character to it.
5. Assign the SkinnedMeshRenderer if required.
6. Assign your GameObject in the AfterimageController component.
7. You can use/copy demo materials and modify its properties to customize the look of them. 
8. Do not hesitate to reach out to me via email with questions/feedback.

Make sure you have Visual Effect Graph installed in your project
